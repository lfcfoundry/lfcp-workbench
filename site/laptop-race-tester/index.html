What is measured

Two public Wikimedia pageview hours joined (H4, 15.3 million rows) and the NASA exoplanet table (H6, 6,473 rows by 703 columns), fetched by this page and kept in browser storage. Each run starts from a fresh page. Workbench is timed from import start until its table is usable and saved as a view; its stored copy finishes later and is reported on its own line. DuckDB-WASM (base build, plus a self-built threaded build, labeled estimated performance) imports the file, adds the first-letter column, filters, sorts, summarizes and writes Parquet. Runs alternate, the order swaps each round, and the threaded runs go last because they can stall on some machines. The parse-thread sweep runs Workbench with 2, 4, 6 and 8 reading threads (8 is the most Workbench uses).
